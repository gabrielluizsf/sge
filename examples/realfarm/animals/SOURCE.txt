Changes made to the models (CC-BY requires saying so):
- All: rendered to 2D sprites in eight facings, lit and scaled for this game.
- Cow: kept its bones; a walk and a grazing motion were written on them.
- Sheep, wolf: their only animation (morph weights) was not used; a skeleton
  of 4 legs, spine, neck, head and tail was added in code, skin weights taken
  from the nearest bones, and walk, graze (sheep) and bite and fall (wolf)
  motions written on it.
- Horse: the model holds two horses side by side; they were separated into
  two coats. The harness's straps, near black in the model, were dyed the
  colour of leather. The model's own walk is used.
